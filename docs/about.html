    About Myanmar FoRB Network

    An independent, inclusive civil society network

    The Myanmar Freedom of Religion or Belief Network (Myanmar FoRB Network) is an independent, inclusive civil society network established in 2021 shortly after the February 2021 military coup in Myanmar.

    It works to promote and protect the right to freedom of religion or belief for everyone, in a situation where civic space has been shrinking drastically, with high risk for human rights defenders and FoRB advocates.

    Who we bring together

    • Faith leaders of different religions
    • Human rights defenders
    • Civil society actors
    • Researchers
    • Journalists
    • Diverse religious & ethnic backgrounds

    Our Vision

    We envision a nation of Myanmar where the right to freedom of thought, conscience, and religion is fully respected by the State and non-state actors and protected from illegitimate and unjust restrictions, discrimination, hostility, and violence based on religious and religion-affiliated identities in any circumstances, including the event of armed conflicts and war.
    Myanmar FoRB Network
    What We Do

    Human rights work focused on FoRB

    Myanmar Freedom of Religion or Belief Network conducts the following human rights work, focusing on FoRB.

    A 01 · Documentation

    Documentation

    Myanmar FoRB Network documents violations of FoRB, particularly the deaths of faith leaders which are directly or implicitly associated with the conflict context in the aftermath of the coup, the abduction, arrest, and prosecution of faith leaders, damage, restrictions, and destruction of places of worship (religious buildings and sites), and the State's illegitimate restriction and prohibition of FoRB practices, and social hostility and harmful hate speech against faith minorities since the February 2021 coup.

    • Deaths of faith leaders
    • Arrest & prosecution
    • Places of worship
    • FoRB restrictions
    • Hate speech
    B 02 · Advocacy

    Advocacy

    Myanmar FoRB Network advocates for FoRB at the regional level and international level. Our advocacy includes presenting updates on the FoRB situation in Myanmar/Burma, based on its research findings and trend and pattern analysis at regional and international conferences and workshops; engaging with and submitting reports of FoRB violations to the UN Special Rapporteur on the human rights situation in Myanmar and the UN Special Rapporteur on Freedom of Religion or Belief; and coordination and collaboration with national stakeholders and relevant human rights organisations and individuals on FoRB issues in Myanmar context; and raising awareness of FoRB and capacity-building activities for documenting FoRB violations in an ethical, systematic and survivor-centred way.

    • UN SR on Myanmar
    • UN SR on FoRB
    • Regional & international forums
    • National stakeholders
    • Capacity-building
    Network Sustainability

    More than five years, despite high vulnerability

    As a loose network working on FoRB, Myanmar FoRB Network has passed through more than five years despite high vulnerability and limitations escalated by the coup and political violence, mainly perpetrated by the Myanmar military.

    The network has routinely and consistently monitored, shared, discussed, and updated the FoRB situation among network members and occasionally organised knowledge-sharing sessions and skill-building training sessions for the members as well.

    2021

    The network is established shortly after the February 2021 military coup.

    2021–24

    Routine monitoring, sharing and discussion of the FoRB situation among members, with knowledge-sharing and skill-building sessions.

    2025

    First Network Strengthening and Strategic Planning Meeting: members develop the network ToRs, adopt crucial policies and discuss ongoing FoRB and intersectional issues.

    2026

    More than 30 members, both individuals and organisations.

    While membership in the Myanmar FoRB Network represents a variety of religions, beliefs, ethnicities, geographic areas, and contexts, safety and security for all members of the network is always the network's priority in the midst of escalating state-sponsored discrimination, armed conflicts, and oppression of freedom of religion or belief.
